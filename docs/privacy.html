Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 25, 2026

clipper runs on its owner's computer. It has no servers and collects no data from anyone.

  • What it stores: when the owner signs in to TikTok or YouTube, the sign-in tokens those services return are saved only on the owner's computer, so clipper can post to the owner's own account. They are never sent anywhere else.
  • What it reads: basic profile information (name and username) and the posting options TikTok reports for the account, only to show who is posting and which privacy settings are allowed.
  • What it sends: the videos and captions the owner chooses to post, sent directly to the service they are posted on.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold or shared with third parties.
  • Deleting: deleting the saved token files, or removing clipper's access in the TikTok or Google account settings, removes all access.

Questions: open an issue on GitHub.
